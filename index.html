About Me

My name is Hussein Adam Hussein Idris. I am single and currently in my second year at Omdurman College of Journalism, Printing, and Technology.

My family consists of four members: my parents, my sister, and me. I was born and raised in Darfur but was unable to complete my education there due to tribal conflicts. Because of this, I was sent to White Nile State with a relative to continue my studies.

I attended Al-Zubair Ibn Al-Awam Primary School and then Al-Hijra Boys' Secondary School, where I successfully completed my secondary education. After that, I enrolled in computer and English courses, but I was unable to complete them due to the ongoing conflicts in Sudan.
